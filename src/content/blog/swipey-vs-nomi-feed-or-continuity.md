Swipey AI vs Nomi is a useful comparison when deciding how to spend a short conversation session. Do you want to explore new character premises, or continue a fictional relationship you already started? Those goals give browsing and continuity different value.

Official information was reviewed on October 8, 2026. This article proposes an original non-explicit selection exercise with adult fictional characters. It does not report catalog rankings, generated samples or measured memory performance.

## Define the session before opening a feed

Suppose your topic is an imaginary mountain café. For discovery, you want to find a character whose premise fits that setting. For continuity, you want the same fictional host to continue planning the café's seasonal menu over several visits.

Set a small browsing limit, such as reviewing three relevant cards before starting a conversation. Without a limit, the comparison can become a measure of time spent scrolling rather than the usefulness of the character you eventually choose.

## Identify the type of companion

Swipey distinguishes platform, verified-creator and custom companions. Inspect which type a card represents before treating its image and description as an original fictional premise. An AI representation associated with a creator is not a live conversation with that real person, and it is different from a private character you design yourself.

Nomi documents Backstory+ for important context. For an ongoing fictional café host, retain a compact setup and an external note of the current menu decision. Judge the actual continuation rather than assuming the presence of a context field guarantees recall.

| Routine | Useful evidence |
|---|---|
| Browse first | A small shortlist relevant to the chosen premise |
| Design first | Supported controls for an original character |
| Continue later | The intended character and thread are easy to reopen |
| Maintain a project | The next agreed task is still understandable |
| Limit attention | You can end without another round of browsing |

## Separate finding a match from keeping it

Ask the selected character to suggest one seasonal menu item and explain its connection to the fictional mountain setting. Assess relevance and manageable reply length. A compelling card may still lead to dialogue that does not fit your topic.

Then close the session after agreeing on a next step. On return, ask what remains to be decided. Record whether it recalls the project, needs a recap or invents a decision you never made. Discovery quality and return quality belong in separate notes.

## Compare the access route you would actually use

Check the current account conditions for the selected companion type. A general subscription description should not be assumed to cover every creator offering or optional media feature. Keep spending on extras separate from the decision about conversation fit.
