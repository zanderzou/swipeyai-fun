<p class="article-lede">OurDream AI now presents Explore, Feed, Community, Create and Generate. The easy claim that Swipey AI has a feed while OurDream does not is wrong. The better comparison is what happens after discovery: the origin and access rules of a Swipey companion, versus the path from OurDream character settings to chat, image and video production.</p>

## Start from discovery, not a feature checklist

Swipey's Explorer Feed links media cards to platform-created characters, AI personas associated with verified creators, and private Custom AI. The official distinctions matter: a creator-linked persona has a different access story from a private character you build yourself, and the real creator is not necessarily typing your AI messages.

OurDream also offers a feed and community discovery, then a creation area for appearance, personality and voice. Compare how long it takes to find a promising character on each service, whether its profile tells you enough about origin and boundaries, and whether you can find it again later. Count taps and time, not just thumbnail quantity.

## Run separate chat and image tests

Use the same fictional adult profile and a harmless prompt: a character planning a museum visit in a green jacket. Chat for six turns, change the subject, and return to the plan. Record exact recall and initiative. Then make two non-explicit images changing only the background. Score facial consistency separately from conversational continuity; a service can be strong on one and weak on the other.

For Swipey, remember that the official FAQ says a private Custom AI requires Premium to chat. For OurDream, check what can be customized and saved on your current plan. Neither product's marketing description proves a measured memory or image success rate. This publication has not run that comparative benchmark.

## Work out the cost of a usable sequence

OurDream describes subscriptions and DreamCoins; Swipey separates Premium, Hearts and potentially Fan Club. Internal units should not be compared as if they share an exchange rate. Price one concrete sequence in your local currency: a week of chat, two usable images, and one short video or voice interaction. Include failed attempts, regenerated outputs, exports and renewal terms.

Do not infer that an advertised unlimited tier covers every image or video action. Read the current checkout and feature conditions, as allowances and bundles change.

## Rights, privacy and final choice

Use a clearly adult fictional person and your own or authorized source images. If you share a created character, examine visibility and deletion controls. For private chats and media on both services, read current data handling policies rather than assuming that "private" means no provider-side processing.

Choose Swipey AI for closer evaluation when verified-creator personas and Fan Club are the reason you browse. Choose OurDream AI for closer evaluation when you want to design an appearance and narrative, then make images or video from it. Both have feeds; your result quality, total cost and control of personal data should decide.
