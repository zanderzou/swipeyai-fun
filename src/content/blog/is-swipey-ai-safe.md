<p class="article-lede">Swipey AI is a real adult AI companion service with a named operator, published terms, privacy documents, support contact, and account controls. That does not make intimate chat confidential or risk-free. Its policies describe processing user content and technical data, voice handling, moderation, recurring subscriptions, and data retention. Use it like any other online entertainment service: minimize what you disclose, understand the bill, and keep fictional interaction separate from real-world decisions.</p>

| Area | What the official documents say | Safer habit |

## Who operates Swipey AI?

The official privacy policy and terms identify INVAI LTD, a company registered in Cyprus, as the operator. The documents provide a registration number, postal address, and support contacts. This is useful accountability information, but users should still verify that the pages are current before registering or paying.

Use the official domain directly. Avoid unofficial download pages, cloned login screens, direct-message “support” accounts, or anyone asking for a password, payment code, identity document, or remote access outside the service's stated process.

## What data can Swipey AI process?

The privacy policy describes account and registration information, usage information, browser and device data, IP and location-related technical information, communications, cookies, and Content supplied through the platform. Its definition of Content includes discussions with AI companions.

That means the safest assumption is simple: messages are data handled by an online service. Do not disclose home or work addresses, legal names tied to other identifiers, financial details, passwords, health records, employer information, real relationship disputes, or another person's private information.

Use a fictional profile for roleplay. A scenario does not become less immersive because the city, job, or personal history is invented.

## Voice calls and microphone privacy

Swipey AI's current terms say its voice-call feature uses speech recognition and voice synthesis and requires microphone access. They also describe voice input processing for service delivery, moderation, security, quality, and legal compliance, with more detail delegated to the applicable policies.

Before a call:

- use browser-level microphone permission rather than granting broad device access;
- close other applications that might produce private audio;
- avoid speaking names, addresses, financial information, or third-party secrets;
- revoke microphone permission when you are finished;
- check whether the current interface offers history or voice-data deletion.

Synthetic voice can feel immediate and personal, but it is still an online feature processing audio—not a private telephone call.

## Retention and account deletion

The official privacy policy describes retaining personal information while an account exists or while needed for stated purposes. It also says information can remain for a period after account closure and describes rights to access, correct, restrict, object, withdraw consent, and request erasure, subject to legal exceptions.

Read the live wording before creating an account because retention terms can change. If deletion matters, first remove chats or media where controls exist, save only non-sensitive billing records you need, cancel the subscription, request account deletion, and retain the confirmation. Deleting a browser cookie is not the same as deleting a server-side account.

## Age assurance and adult boundaries

Swipey AI's current terms describe the service as strictly for adults aged 18 or the higher age of majority that applies locally. They also discuss age assurance and prohibit material involving minors or people who appear to be minors.

Keep every depicted and described person clearly adult. Avoid youth-coded labels, school-age framing, ambiguous ages, or attempts to bypass safeguards. Consent matters even when media is synthetic: do not imitate, sexualize, or manipulate the likeness or voice of a real person without explicit permission.

## Moderation and flagged conversations

The current terms describe layered moderation: input blocking, model-level controls, output checks, and potential review of flagged inputs or outputs by compliance staff. The practical implication is that “uncensored” marketing should never be interpreted as “unmonitored” or “anything is allowed.”

Follow the provider's current acceptable-use rules and the law in your location. Do not attempt to evade age, safety, moderation, or access controls.

## Creator Models and impersonation awareness

Swipey AI markets Verified Creator Models as AI versions associated with consenting adult contributors, while its terms distinguish AI-generated interaction from real human interaction. Users should understand when a conversation is synthetic, when content is creator-supplied, what a Fanclub includes, and whether a custom request involves the AI system or a human creator.

Never send money, credentials, identity documents, or private images because a character asks. AI characters cannot make real commitments, arrange an authentic relationship, or provide trustworthy financial, legal, medical, or emergency advice.

## Billing, tokens, and renewal

The terms describe paid subscriptions, tokens for extended features, automatic renewal, cancellation, and refund limits, while the public pricing page can change. Before checkout, record:

1. the billing period and total charged now;
2. the renewal price and renewal date;
3. included messages, tokens, hearts, calls, images, video, and Fanclub access;
4. whether unused balances expire;
5. how add-on purchases work;
6. the cancellation route and refund terms.

Set a monthly entertainment cap before using progression systems or creator-linked purchases. Daily rewards do not automatically make paid actions inexpensive.

## A safer-use checklist

- Open only the official `swipey.ai` domain.
- Use a unique password and email alias where appropriate.
- Keep profile, backstory, and roleplay details fictional.
- Share no passwords, codes, financial details, private photos, or third-party secrets.
- Grant microphone permission only for the call you intend to make.
- Treat all characters as synthetic entertainment, not real partners or advisers.
- Verify tokens, hearts, renewal, cancellation, and refunds before paying.
- Review deletion and retention wording before sensitive use.
- Stop and seek real human help for health, safety, legal, financial, or crisis issues.

## Verdict

Swipey AI publishes meaningful operator, privacy, billing, age, and moderation information, which is better than using an anonymous service with no policies. The documents also make clear that user data, chats, voice interactions, and billing require attention.

“Safe” is not a permanent badge. Recheck the official policies, use fictional information, control microphone access, understand retention, and keep spending and emotional expectations deliberate.
