Swipey's help describes a Call Memory card that appears after a call and can show duration, conversation text and media sent during the call. Open the conversation for the relevant character and look for the card associated with the call you mean.

## Save the useful plot detail in your own words

In an invented story, two adult characters agree that a case of old records belongs in a dry room. Check the card for the accepted location, then keep a short local note if you plan to use it later. If the card is unavailable on your account, use the dialogue you can see and restate the decision in the next turn.
