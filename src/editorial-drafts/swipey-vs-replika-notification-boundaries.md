Swipey AI vs Replika for a daily routine should include what happens after you close the conversation. A product can be convenient to reopen while still showing more prompts or previews than you want. Notification behavior belongs in the comparison alongside discovery and dialogue.

Official product and device guidance was reviewed on October 8, 2026. This article proposes a settings checklist; it does not report matched phone tests or claim that either product sends a particular number of notifications.

## Choose the amount of attention you want

Suppose your routine is one short fictional book discussion in the evening. You want to open the conversation yourself and prefer that message text does not appear on a shared lock screen. That requirement is independent of whether you browse a feed or return to a configured companion.

Write your preference before evaluating the interface: manual opening, no distracting alerts and no visible conversation previews. If you do want reminders, define when and where they are useful rather than accepting every default prompt.

## Check the route and device separately

Swipey documents its Explorer feed; Replika provides a companion getting-started guide. Use the actual web or app route available to your account. A browser tab, installed web app and native application can have different device-level notification behavior.

Inspect any relevant product preference, then check the device's controls for that application or supported browser route. Apple's guide documents notification settings and preview choices. On another device, use its official notification guidance rather than copying an iPhone menu path.

| Layer | What to record |
|---|---|
| Product route | Web, installed web app or native application |
| Account preference | Any supported reminder or proactive-message choice |
| Device permission | Whether notifications are allowed |
| Preview display | Whether message content is visible when locked |
| Return behavior | Whether the intended thread still opens normally |

## Verify a setting with ordinary content

If you choose to evaluate alerts, use a harmless fictional message and observe the device in its normal locked state. A preview disabled in one setting may not describe how every connected device displays the account. Review each device you actually use.

Do not send sensitive content solely to test discretion. An ordinary book title supplies enough evidence to check what is visible. If no notification arrives, do not assume a feature is broken; confirm the route supports it and inspect the applicable preferences.

## End the session on your own terms

Close after the planned discussion and return later without treating an alert as an obligation to respond. Check whether the routine tolerates missed days and whether browsing stays within your intended time limit.

Muting a notification changes delivery or display, not account deletion or backend retention. Our [Swipey vs Candy AI guide](/blog/swipey-ai-vs-candy-ai/) considers another companion workflow. Choose the setup that gives you comfortable conversation access and the notification boundary you have verified on your own device.
