**A data-copy request is best considered before a permanent profile action removes ordinary account access.** Swipey's [data guide](https://swipey.ai/blog/ai-companion-data-privacy) describes requesting information and separately deleting the profile. Decide which records you need rather than assuming a deleted account can later reproduce them on demand.

## Define the copy you are requesting

The [privacy policy](https://swipey.ai/privacy-policy) describes a personal-data access request to its Controller. A copy of personal data and a convenient, app-importable conversation backup are not necessarily the same deliverable.

State the categories you need and ask what format is available. Do not promise yourself a complete restoration file unless the provider actually confirms one. Preserve the request reference and the response, including any stated limits.

Avoid gathering more sensitive material merely to make a larger archive. Keep any received copy in a location you control, and decide separately what should remain there.

## Keep billing and balance explicit

The provider's deletion guide says Premium should be canceled first to avoid another billing period. Confirm that billing state separately from the content-removal request.

The [Hearts FAQ](https://swipey.ai/faq/hearts-billing/do-hearts-expire) distinguishes an active account's purchased balance from deletion, which removes remaining Hearts. A retained balance is therefore not an account-restoration promise. You do not need to spend it or make another purchase to document the state before deciding what to do.

## Read the retention statement accurately

Section 12 of the policy describes a usual six-year personal-information retention period after account closure and says periods can change with business or regulatory requirements. Other policy sections address erasure requests and possible legal exceptions.

This is the provider's published wording, not an independent determination that every conversation must be retained for exactly six years. Ask which categories, purposes and periods apply to your particular request. Likewise, retained information does not necessarily form an archive that can restore a deleted user account.

## Keep the confirmations separate

For an illustrative sequence, a request acknowledgment arrives first, a data response follows and a later message confirms profile removal. Those are three events. An acknowledgment alone should not be presented as a completed copy or deletion.

If the provider retains some categories, preserve its explanation. If a response is incomplete, identify the missing category rather than repeatedly submitting an ambiguous request for everything.

We have not submitted a request or deleted a profile. The useful outcome is a documented scope: what copy was supplied, what billing state was confirmed, what access was removed and what information the provider says remains. That is more precise than assuming a vanished login means every record everywhere was immediately erased.
